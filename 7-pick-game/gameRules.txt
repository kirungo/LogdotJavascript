1. Roll the dice: The active player rolls one die. If they roll 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6, that value is added to their current score.









2. If you roll a 1

Your current score resets to zero, and your turn ends. Any points accumulated during that turn are lost.












3. Decide whether to roll again

After rolling 2 to 6, you can keep rolling to accumulate more points, but risk losing your current turn's points if you roll a 1.




4. Hold

Add your current score to your total score. Your current score resets to zero, and the other player takes their turn.




5. Win the game

The first player to reach 100 points wins.
